This wiki is written and maintained by Claude from the documents and notes you give it.
It is kept in a private, encrypted place of its own, apart from every other business, and
only you can open it, through your oeru sign-in.

## Start here

- [[overview]] — what the business is and the list of projects
- [[how-this-wiki-works]] — how to add documents and notes

## Adding to the wiki

Click **Upload** at the top right of any page and drop files onto it:

- **Documents** (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, scans).
- **Notes and decisions** from a Claude conversation (Update Packets, `.md` files).

The Upload page shows each file going up and a live line of what the wiki is doing while
it reads them. The pages here change once the reading is done.
