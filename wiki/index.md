## Adding to the wiki

- **Documents** (PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images): drop them into **Wiki Intake** on
  the Desktop.
- **Notes and decisions** from a Claude conversation (Update Packets, `.md` files): drop
  them into **Wiki Inbox** on the Desktop.

A notification appears when the wiki has been updated. Refresh this page to see it.
