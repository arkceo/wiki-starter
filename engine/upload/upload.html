          Settings

          Reading speed

          Performance mode

          The mode sets three figures for you: how many documents go in one batch, the largest file the Upload page takes, and the spending cap per batch. A document costs the same in every mode: the cap is a ceiling that stops a batch costing more than expected, and the documents it did not reach wait for the next run. Changes apply from the next batch.

          Review questions

          Auto-review only answers questions the documents themselves settle, when Jev is at least 70% sure: Jev checks that first. A question that needs your own knowledge or decision (which terms you agreed, what a photo is for) always waits for you, however confident Jev is. Every decision Jev makes is listed in the Review tab, where you can change it. A file is never deleted: the most auto-review does with one is set it aside in raw/_set-aside/.

          Jev, the review judge (TypeSafe)

          With a key, each review question, its options, and the parts of the wiki and the document it is about are sent to TypeSafe to be scored. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent without a key. The key is kept in this Mac's Keychain, not in a file. Get one at console.typesafe.ai.
