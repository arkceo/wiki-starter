      Jev, the review judge (TypeSafe)

      With a key, each review question, its options, and the parts of the wiki and the document it is about are sent to TypeSafe to be scored. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent without a key. The key is kept in this Mac's Keychain, not in a file. Get one at console.typesafe.ai.
